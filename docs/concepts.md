# Concepts

## The three pillars

3psLCCA quantifies bridge life cycle impacts across three pillars, all expressed in a common
monetary metric so that alternatives can be compared directly:

| Pillar | Captures |
| --- | --- |
| Economic | Direct financial costs: construction, operation, inspection, maintenance, rehabilitation, hazard-induced repair, decommissioning |
| Environmental | Impacts such as carbon emissions, converted to monetary terms |
| Social | User-related costs, e.g. disruption from lane closures, delays, and detours |

## Life cycle stages

A bridge assessment in 3psLCCA is broken down by stage:

- **Construction** - initial build cost and embodied impacts.
- **Operation & inspection** - routine monitoring costs over the service life.
- **Maintenance** - scheduled upkeep.
- **Rehabilitation** - major interventions to restore performance.
- **Hazard-induced repair** - repairs following earthquakes or other extreme events.
- **Decommissioning** - end-of-life removal/disposal.

## Regional databases

3psLCCA ships with a default database calibrated for India, covering material, labor, and
process costs typical of steel and PSC girder short-span bridges. Users can define
project-specific parameters or import a database for another region, and the modular
architecture is intended to extend to additional bridge typologies and span ranges over time.
